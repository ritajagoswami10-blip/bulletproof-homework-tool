# Research & Academic Writing 📖

Guides for research papers, literature reviews, and academic work.

## Research Paper Structure

### I. Introduction
- Hook
- Background
- Literature gap
- Thesis/Research question
- Scope and limitations

### II. Literature Review
- Summary of existing research
- Identification of themes
- Gaps in knowledge

### III. Methodology
- Research design
- Data collection methods
- Analysis approach

### IV. Results/Findings
- Presentation of data
- Figures and tables
- Key findings

### V. Discussion
- Interpretation of results
- Comparison with literature
- Limitations
- Implications

### VI. Conclusion
- Summary of findings
- Broader implications
- Future research directions

### VII. References
- All sources cited in proper format

## Citation Management

### Tools
- **Zotero** - Free, open-source
- **Mendeley** - Integrated reference manager
- **EndNote** - Professional tool
- **Google Scholar** - Free source finder

### Citation Formats

#### APA (7th Edition)
```
Author, A. A. (2024). Title of work. Publisher.
```

#### MLA (9th Edition)
```
Author. "Title of Work." Publisher, 2024.
```

#### Chicago (17th Edition)
```
Author. Title of Work. Publisher, 2024.
```

## Research Tips

✅ Use credible sources  
✅ Take organized notes  
✅ Cite as you write  
✅ Use multiple sources  
✅ Check for bias  
✅ Peer review  
✅ Plagiarism check
