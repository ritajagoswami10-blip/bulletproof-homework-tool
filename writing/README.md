# Writing Assignments ✍️

Templates and guides for essays, reports, and academic writing.

## Assignment Types

1. **Essays** - Persuasive, analytical, descriptive
2. **Research Papers** - Academic research with citations
3. **Lab Reports** - Scientific experiment documentation
4. **Reflective Writing** - Personal reflection and analysis
5. **Creative Writing** - Stories, poetry, creative pieces

## Essay Structure

### Introduction
- Hook to grab attention
- Background information
- Thesis statement

### Body Paragraphs
- Topic sentence
- Evidence/examples
- Explanation
- Link back to thesis

### Conclusion
- Restate thesis
- Summarize main points
- Final thought or call to action

## Writing Tips

✅ Use clear, concise language  
✅ Show, don't tell  
✅ Vary sentence structure  
✅ Proofread multiple times  
✅ Get feedback from others  
✅ Follow the required format (APA, MLA, etc.)  

## Citation Formats

### APA (Author, Year)
- Used in social sciences
- (Smith, 2024)

### MLA (Author, Page)
- Used in humanities
- (Smith 42)

### Chicago (Footnotes/Endnotes)
- Used in history
- Superscript numbers with references
